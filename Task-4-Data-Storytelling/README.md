## Business Insights
- Total Customers: 2240
- Average Income: ₹52,247
- Average Spending: ₹605.8
- Campaign Response Rate: 14.91%
- Higher income customers spend significantly more.

## Statistical Validation
Test Used: Independent Two Sample T-Test

T Statistic: 52.964

P Value: 0.000

Decision:
Reject Null Hypothesis.

## Conclusion
Income has a statistically significant impact on customer spending. Marketing campaigns should focus on high-value customer segments.
